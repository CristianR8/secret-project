Who are we?

Computer Vision & AI Lab (CVAIL) is a research group investigating robust perception, scene understanding, and decision-making for intelligent systems in complex environments.

Our work combines physical models, mathematics, and deep learning to analyze complex scenes.

Our research advances machine perception through computer vision, computational imaging, and artificial intelligence. We study how visual information is captured, represented, and interpreted by intelligent systems.
